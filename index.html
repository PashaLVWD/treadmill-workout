About screen-awake mode
During a workout, this app will try to prevent your screen from locking. Keep it visible; iOS may override the screen-awake request, and voice alerts may stop if the screen locks. Treadmill adjustments are manual.

    Safety: V2 is the more demanding workout; use it no more than once weekly initially, with easier sessions between. Reduce speed or incline if your breathing becomes uncontrolled or if you need the handrails to support your weight. Stop for chest pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath.
